04 / 06Corridors

Neighborhoods of Lusaka

Security, schooling, and proximity to embassies and the diplomatic corridor drive most of our buyer conversations before price ever comes up. Here's how the four we work in most compare.

Kabulonga

Lusaka's traditional diplomatic address. Wide plots, mature jacaranda-lined streets, and the highest concentration of embassies and high-commissions in the city.

SecurityGated, 24hr patrol standard
SchoolsICS, American Int'l, 5–10 min
Corporate15+ embassies within 3km
CharacterEstablished, low turnover

Ibex Hill

Elevated, newer-build corridor favoured by senior executives. Larger plots than Kabulonga at a comparable price point, with faster access to the CBD since the recent road upgrades.

SecurityEstate-managed, boomed access
SchoolsRhodes Park, 10–15 min
Corporate10 min to Manda Hill / CBD
CharacterNew-build, larger footprints

Roma

Quiet, family-first, and the shortest run to the international-school belt. Slightly smaller plots than Kabulonga or Ibex Hill, traded for walkability and a tighter-knit street feel.

SecurityNeighbourhood watch, low crime
SchoolsLusaka Int'l, walkable
CorporateMixed residential, low traffic
CharacterTree-lined, family-oriented

Sunningdale

The value corridor of the four: newer housing stock at a materially lower entry price, popular with first-generation buyers and as a rental-yield play for investors.

SecurityPerimeter walls, private guards
SchoolsTwin Palm, 10 min
CorporateEmerging retail corridor
CharacterNewer stock, strong yield
